The practice, in words

Choose one of three sections and a speed: 0.75× or 1×. Watch the teacher, then hear “Ready. Make room.” and take five seconds to get into position. “Your turn” starts a timed practice window. The lesson returns automatically, for three rounds of each section. The remote pauses picture, voice and the practice timer together. Your section and speed stay saved on the TV.

Watch it. Make room. Try it.

  1. 1

    Watch once

    Pick a short section. See the step at 1× or slow it to 0.75×.

  2. 2

    Take five seconds

    Hear “Ready. Make room.” A clear countdown gives you time to take your place.

  3. 3

    Your turn

    Hear “Your turn” and work through the step yourself, even with your eyes away from the screen.

  4. 4

    Go again

    The lesson returns automatically. Three sections, three rounds each.

Need a breather? Pause picture and voice with the remote. TV volume controls the cues. Your last section and speed stay on the TV, ready for the next practice.
